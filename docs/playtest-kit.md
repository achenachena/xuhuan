# First-player playtest kit

Status: not yet conducted. No player counts or engagement results have been collected by this change.

## Invite

> Could you try a 90-second browser game I built? You do not need Telegram or an account. I would like to watch where the game explains itself poorly, so I will not explain it beforehand. You can stop at any time. Is it okay if I take anonymous notes? https://xuhuan-miniapp.vercel.app/demo

Ask permission separately before recording anyone's screen or voice. Written notes are sufficient. Do not collect names, Telegram IDs or contact details in the public repository.

## Observe, without coaching

Recruit five people unfamiliar with the game. Aim for a mix of desktop and phone users, including people who do not know the characters. Do not explain the core mechanic before the attempt and do not require a second attempt.

Record time to first movement; whether the first reversal is noticed; how the player explains the weapon choice; where they stop; and whether they choose replay before being prompted. Afterward ask only:

1. Where were you confused?
2. What moment do you remember?
3. Would you play again, and why?

## Decision gates

- First five: at least four start unaided and three describe the reversal. If not, revise the first ten seconds before broad posting.
- First outreach cycle: aim for ten unfamiliar participants, five concrete feedback items and three unprompted replays. These are goals, not reported results or guaranteed outcomes.
- Record a concrete change and retest the same comprehension question with new players. Do not call a five-person convenience sample a statistically validated retention study.
- Website visits are not unique players. Use direct observation for participant/replay counts and GitHub Traffic for repository visits/referrers, with date windows clearly recorded.

## Two-week sequence

Days 1–2: baseline sessions. Days 3–6: review the delivered opening/result changes against observations. Days 7–9: check the engineering evidence and media. Days 10–14: post to one suitable channel at a time and respond to feedback. Dates depend on actual participant availability.

Keep applications moving while collecting feedback. Do not postpone job applications until a star target is reached.
